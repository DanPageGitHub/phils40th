★ Where You'll Sleep

Camping + Sleep

Three Pools has proper camping, not a festival mudpit. Everyone stays on site. Hot showers and clean toilets. Bring a good sleeping bag, October nights in the Black Mountains can be cold. Contact us about accessibility or sensory needs.

⛺
Camping Fields (Included in Ticket)

Flat grass pitches, fire pits, hot showers, and proper toilets. Bring your tent and a solid sleeping bag.

🚐
Live-In Vehicles (Included in Ticket)

Space on site for campervans and motorhomes. Select the live-in vehicle option when you buy your ticket.

♿

★ Farm to Plate

Food + Drink

Welcome meal (Friday) and farewell breakfast (Sunday) included in ticket price. Three Pools grows its own food on site. Fresh farm produce sold throughout the weekend, with vegan and gluten free options. Self-catering welcome, (Hot water urn and hob facilities on site) BYOB for campsite sesh.

🌿
Farm-Grown Food on Site
