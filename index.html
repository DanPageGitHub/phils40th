★ Where You'll Sleep

Camping + Sleep

Three Pools has proper camping, not a festival mudpit. Everyone stays on site. Hot showers and clean toilets. Bring a good sleeping bag, October nights in the Black Mountains can be cold. Contact us about accessibility or sensory needs.

⛺
Camping Fields (Included in Ticket)

Flat grass pitches, fire pits, hot showers, and proper toilets. Bring your tent and a solid sleeping bag.

🚐
Live-In Vehicles Welcome

Space on site for campervans and motorhomes. Register your vehicle when you buy your ticket. Contact us about accessibility or sensory needs.

🔔
10 Bell Tents, Book Direct

10 bell tents at Three Pools, 4-berth, £250 per tent. Pre-pitched, beds and bedding included. Once they're gone they're gone. Book direct at threepools.co.uk/event-details/phils-40th-accommodation

💧
Sauna

On-site sauna. Available Saturday and Sunday mornings. Essential.

Good to know
Pack for cold nights

October in the Black Mountains. Bring layers. The rave barn will be warm, the walk back at 4am will not.

No day visitors

Everyone stays on site for the full weekend. It keeps the headcount right and the vibe right.

No kids or dogs

Sorry, adults only.
